⚠ UDP connectivity issue

One or more nodes report UDP as degraded. Ensure both TCP and UDP are forwarded and allowed through your router and firewall. Check provider firewalls, double NAT and VPN/tunnel forwarding. After fixing, it can take about an hour for health status to update.

A failed request can mean the port is closed, the service is unavailable, or the request timed out. It does not prove that the node itself is down.
